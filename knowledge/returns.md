# Returns, refunds and cancellations

## Q: What is your return policy?
You can return most items within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. Items must be unused
and in the original packaging. Sale items marked final sale cannot be returned. Start a
return from your account, or ask us on this call and we will email you a prepaid label.

## Q: How long do refunds take?
Refunds are issued within 2 business days of the warehouse receiving your return. Card
issuers then take 3 to 5 business days to post the credit. If it has been more than 7
business days since we issued the refund, ask us to re-issue it on this call.

## Q: Can I cancel my order?
Orders can be cancelled for free until they enter the packing stage, usually within 2 hours
of ordering. After that, refuse the delivery or start a return once it arrives. Cancellations
are refunded in full to the original payment method.

## Q: Do I have to pay for return shipping?
No. Returns in the US and Canada include a prepaid label. International returns pay a flat
$12 return fee, deducted from the refund unless the item arrived damaged.

## Q: What if my item arrived damaged?
Send us a photo by replying to your order email, or ask us on this call to open a damage
ticket. Damaged items are replaced or refunded within 5 business days with no return required
for items under $50.
