# Orders and billing

## Q: How can I check my order status?
We can check it right now on this call. Tell us your order number (format ORD-1001) and we
will read out the current status, the estimated delivery date and the tracking number.
Unknown numbers return a not-found error, so double check the digits.

## Q: What payment methods do you accept?
We accept Visa, Mastercard, Amex, Discover, Apple Pay, Google Pay and PayPal. Business
accounts on a credit plan can also pay by invoice with net-30 terms.

## Q: When am I charged for an order?
Cards are authorised at checkout and charged when the order ships. For pre-orders we charge
a 20 percent deposit at checkout and the balance on the ship date.

## Q: I was charged twice
Duplicate authorisations drop off automatically within 3 business days. If you see two
actual captures, ask us on this call to open a billing ticket and we will refund the extra
charge within 1 business day.

## Q: Do you offer price adjustments?
Yes. If an item you bought in the last 14 days goes on sale, call us and we will refund
the difference to your original payment method. One adjustment per item.
