# Plans, pricing and store information

## Q: What are your support hours?
Phone support is open Monday to Friday, 8 AM to 8 PM ET, and Saturday 9 AM to 5 PM ET.
Chat and email are staffed 24 hours a day, every day. Calls outside phone hours can still
open tickets for a next-business-day callback.

## Q: Where are you located?
Our warehouse is in Columbus, Ohio, and our support centre is in Austin, Texas. We do not
have retail stores; everything is sold online with delivery and returns by courier.

## Q: Do you have a student or military discount?
Students get 10 percent off with a verified .edu email address at checkout. Active duty
military, veterans and first responders get 15 percent off after a one-time ID verification.

## Q: How do I contact a human?
Phone support is the fastest route: you are on it now. You can also email
help@acmestore.example or start a chat on the website. Ask us on this call to be connected
to a live agent and we will transfer you right away.

## Q: Do you price match?
We match any authorized US retailer's price on identical in-stock items within 7 days of
your purchase. Call us with the competitor's URL and we will refund the difference.
