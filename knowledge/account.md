# Account and profile

## Q: How do I reset my password?
Go to the sign-in page and choose Forgot password. We email a link that expires in 30
minutes. If the email does not arrive within 5 minutes, check spam, then call us and we can
send a new link or update your email address for you.

## Q: How do I change my email or shipping address?
Signed-in customers can edit details under Account, then Profile. Shipping addresses on
already-placed orders can only be changed before packing starts; call us and we will try to
intercept and update it.

## Q: How do I delete my account?
Call us or email privacy@acmestore.example and we will delete the account within 7 days.
Order history is kept for 7 years for tax purposes, but all marketing and profile data is
removed immediately.

## Q: What is the loyalty program?
Acme Rewards is free to join. Members earn 1 point per dollar, get free standard shipping on
every order, and reach gold tier at 2,000 points a year with early access to sales.
