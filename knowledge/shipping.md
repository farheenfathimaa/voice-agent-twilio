# Shipping and delivery

## Q: How long does shipping take?
Standard shipping takes 3 to 5 business days within the continental US. Expedited shipping
arrives in 2 business days and costs $9.99. Orders placed before 2 PM ET on weekdays ship
the same day. Weekend and holiday orders ship the next business day.

## Q: How do I track my order?
Every shipment gets an email with a tracking number that starts with 1Z. You can also ask
us on this call: give us your order number (it looks like ORD-1001) and we will look up the
live tracking status for you.

## Q: Do you ship internationally?
Yes. We ship to Canada, the UK, the EU and Australia. International delivery takes 7 to 12
business days and duties are calculated at checkout. We do not ship to PO boxes
internationally.

## Q: My package says delivered but I do not have it
Wait 24 hours, then check with neighbors and building mailrooms. If it still has not
appeared, call us back and we will open a replacement ticket immediately, no investigation
wait time is required for orders under $200.

## Q: What are your delivery options?
You can choose standard (3-5 days, free over $50), expedited (2 days, $9.99) or same-day
courier in Manhattan, Brooklyn and San Francisco ($14.99, order before 11 AM local time).
