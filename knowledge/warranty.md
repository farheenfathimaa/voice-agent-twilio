# Warranty and repairs

## Q: What warranty do your products have?
Every product ships with a 2-year limited warranty covering manufacturing defects. Accessories
have a 12-month warranty. The warranty does not cover accidental damage, water damage or
consumable parts like batteries.

## Q: How do I make a warranty claim?
Call us with the order number or the serial number on the box. Approved claims ship a
replacement within 3 business days and we email a return label for the faulty unit. No
repair centre visit is needed for items under $300.

## Q: Can I buy an extended warranty?
Yes. Acme Care+ extends coverage to 4 years for 12 percent of the item price and adds
accidental damage protection with a $29 deductible per claim. It can be added within 30 days
of delivery.

## Q: Do you offer repairs?
Out-of-warranty repairs are handled by our service partners. Flat diagnostic fee is $39,
quoted before any repair. Typical turnaround is 5 to 7 business days after we receive the
unit.
